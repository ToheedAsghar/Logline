Logline Tracker - Installation Instructions

Because this app is currently distributed unsigned (outside the Mac App Store and without an Apple Developer ID), macOS Gatekeeper will block it from opening if you simply double-click it.

To install and run:

1. Drag the LoglineSync app to your Applications folder.
2. Try to open the LoglineSync app by double-clicking or right-clicking and selecting "Open". 
   - macOS will show a warning: "LoglineSync Not Opened — Apple could not verify LoglineSync is free of malware...". 
   - Click "Done".
3. Open **System Settings** on your Mac and go to **Privacy & Security**.
4. Scroll down until you see a message stating that LoglineSync was blocked from use.
5. Click the **Open Anyway** button next to that message.
6. Open the LoglineSync app one more time. 
   - A final confirmation dialog will appear, this time with a real **Open** button. 
   - Click **Open**.

The app will now launch and its icon will appear in your menu bar. 

You only need to do this the very first time you launch the app. After that, you can double-click it normally. You will also be prompted to grant Keychain access on the first run, which you should allow.
